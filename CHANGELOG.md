# Losica 0.26.0

- Lexical semantics are generated as internal `sr:*` regions over structural multilingual colexification evidence.
- Seed 19020 maps 512 semantic probes into 294 generated lexical regions.
- 177 generated regions contain multiple external semantic anchors.
- 43 external probes participate in multiple generated regions.
- Semantic-region identity is internal to Losica; Concepticon identifiers serve as external anchors.
- Lexical class and argument structure are generated after semantic-region formation.
- The empirical compiler projects stable identifiers and numeric topology fields into the causal core.
- English annotations are supplied through `display_en_v0_26.json` after generation.
- Pre-generation annotation scrambling, structural-core reconstruction, adapter-blocking, and display-removal invariance gates are included in the test suite.
- Seed 19020 validates with 629 lexemes, 9224 paradigm cells, 18 construction families, and two capability probes.
